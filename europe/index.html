MARKET PACE — CITY VIEW
Curve shows % of capacity sold by weeks before show date, interpolated onto a standard weekly grid from each tour's actual snapshot reports. Left = furthest from show, right = on-sale day, so every curve should read as climbing left-to-right. Gaps at the left edge mean that tour wasn't yet on sale at that many weeks out — not zero sales. Note the axis runs much further left than the UK/IE tracker: the 2022 EU tour had an unusually long, ~13-month pre-sale window. The 2027 line is now a real curve from seven general-sale reads (2, 6, 13, 20, 27 Oct, 3 and 9 Nov) — still short (it only spans ~5.5 weeks of reporting), so read it alongside the Live Tracker below rather than projecting its slope forward.
TICKETS SOLD — SAME VIEW IN ABSOLUTE NUMBERS
The same weeks-out timeline as the chart above, but in actual tickets rather than % of capacity — useful where a market's % looks slow or fast mainly because its room size has changed tour-on-tour (e.g. Antwerp 1,900 this time vs 180 in 2019). Faint dotted horizontals mark each tour's capacity, the sellout level for that room. Past-tour figures are derived from % sold × that tour's capacity at the time; the 2027 Tour's points are the exact reported counts.

PRE-SALE — DIRECT TO FAN
Pre-sale = cumulative sold at close of business Thu 1 Oct 2026 (windows 30 Sep – 1 Oct) · general sale opened Fri 2 Oct
Pre-sale is tracked as its own metric and sits outside the weekly sell-through curves, which start from general sale. No earlier tour grid separates pre-sale, so this becomes a tour-on-tour comparison from 2027 onwards. "Total Sold" and "General Sale Sold" reflect the latest read (9 Nov); pre-sale itself is a fixed 1 Oct figure.
