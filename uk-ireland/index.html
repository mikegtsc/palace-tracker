MARKET PACE — CITY VIEW
Curve shows % of capacity sold by weeks before show date, interpolated onto a standard weekly grid from each tour's actual snapshot reports. Left = furthest from show, right = on-sale day, so every curve should read as climbing left-to-right. Gaps at the left edge mean that tour wasn't yet on sale at that many weeks out — not zero sales. 2027 Tour is the live tour: one point per end-of-day count (2, 6, 13, 20, 27 and 3 Nov 2026), so its line builds as updates arrive. It went on sale ~31 weeks out versus ~35–39 for earlier tours, so on this axis the earlier comps are already weeks into their sale at the same x — use the Live Tracker below, which compares each market at the same number of days since general sale opened. 2026 Somerset House is a one-off London courtyard show, not a tour stop — it sold out ~13.5 weeks before the show, so its curve stops there and isn't a like-for-like tour comp.
TICKETS SOLD — SAME VIEW IN ABSOLUTE NUMBERS
The same weeks-out axis as above, in tickets rather than % of capacity. Where a market is playing a bigger room than on an earlier tour, a lower % can still be as many or more tickets — read the two charts together. Earlier tours' ticket counts are their % sold × that tour's capacity on the chart above; 2027 shows the exact reported counts.

% of capacity sold at each weeks-out checkpoint, by market and tour. Click a column to sort. 2027 LIVE TRACKER — UK & IE
Each market is compared with the most recent earlier tour that has counts covering the same day since general sale opened (2019: 1 Mar, 2022: 27 Aug 2021, 2024: 1 Mar — confirmed), so lead-time differences drop out. Dublin therefore uses 2022: the 2024 Dublin grid has no counts until 12 Apr 2024. Southampton is new to the tour and has no comp. Capacity differs sharply from the comp in most markets (e.g. London 10,000 vs 5,260), so read “vs comp” alongside “Comp sold at same day” in tickets — a negative % gap in a much bigger room can still be a similar or higher number of tickets. Small or old comps (Newcastle 2019, 650 cap) flatter the 2027 gap. Comp values are interpolated between that tour’s report dates. No projection is made.
PRE-SALE — DIRECT TO FAN
Pre-sale = cumulative sold at close of business Thu 1 Oct 2026 (windows 30 Sep – 1 Oct) · general sale opened Fri 2 Oct
Pre-sale sits outside the weekly sell-through curves, which start from general sale. Comp pre-sale comes from the earlier tour grids: 2024 labels its two pre-sale days (28–29 Feb 2024), so the 29 Feb end-of-day count is used; the 2019 and 2022 grids do not label pre-sale, so the last count before the first on-sale count is used (28 Feb 2019, 26 Aug 2021) — treat those two as inferred. Pre-sale windows and allocations are not recorded in the grids, so a lower figure is not necessarily weaker demand. Leeds 2022 had no pre-sale count reported. “Share of total” is pre-sale as a % of everything sold to date, so it falls as general sale builds.
