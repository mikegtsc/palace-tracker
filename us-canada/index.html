MARKET PACE — CITY VIEW
Curve shows % of capacity sold by weeks before show date, interpolated onto a standard weekly grid from each tour's actual snapshot reports. Weeks before a market's on-sale date are shown as 0%; the 2026 line stops at the most recent count rather than projecting forward. The dashed orange line is a target, not a forecast: the constant weekly rate that would reach 100% sold by show day from today's actual position. Grey dotted verticals mark key moments: "Dream On" (23 Jun), Somerset House (17 Jul), "Ohio" (11 Aug), "Brown Bread" (15 Sep), and the album Ox (18 Sep) — worth watching for any uptick in the weeks after each.
TICKETS SOLD — SAME VIEW IN ABSOLUTE NUMBERS
The same weeks-out timeline as the chart above, but in actual tickets rather than % of capacity — useful where a market looks slow on % because the room is now much bigger than last time. Solid lines are tickets sold; faint dotted horizontals mark each tour's capacity (the sellout level for that room). The dashed orange line is again the straight-line target from today's actual count to a full house on show day — arithmetic, not a forecast. Past-tour totals are derived from each tour's % sold × its capacity at the time.
SELLOUT PACE TARGET — WHERE WE NEED TO ACCELERATE
CITY WKS TO SHOW TIX REMAINING REQUIRED/WK RECENT/WK PACE NEEDED
Pure arithmetic, not a forecast: "Required/wk" = tickets remaining ÷ weeks remaining — the constant rate needed to reach 100% sold by show day. "Recent/wk" = actual average weekly rate over roughly the last 2–3 weeks of reports (smoothed, since day-to-day sales are lumpy). "Pace Needed" = how many times faster than the recent rate would be required — e.g. 2.0x means "sell tickets twice as fast as you have been" to reach a full sellout. Below 1.0x (green) = already on pace for a sellout at the current rate. This assumes a constant rate; it doesn't account for marketing moments ("Dream On", Somerset House, "Ohio", "Brown Bread", the album Ox) potentially lifting the rate — see the city chart above for where those land relative to each show's remaining window.

"VS" columns = current tour's % sold minus the comp tour's % sold, at the same number of weeks before the show. Positive (green) = pacing ahead of that comp market. "CAP CHG VS 2025" = how much bigger/smaller the 2026 room is than the 2025 room in that city — a market can look "behind" on % sold purely because it's now in a much bigger venue (see Washington DC, Salt Lake City, Montreal). ⚠ on the 2024 or Best Older Comp columns flags a lower-confidence source (2024 Mini Tour or 2022 Fall — both had unusual, non-standard routing rather than a normal full on-sale cycle). "BEST OLDER COMP" fills the gap for markets with no 2025 show, trying 2023 Summer and 2022 Spring first before falling back to the lower-confidence tours as a last resort. Blank = no comparable market on any prior tour. New York/Brooklyn and Oakland/San Francisco are each treated as one market.
